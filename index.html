Why Blue?

Blue is often associated with calmness and serenity. It can evoke feelings of trust and reliability, making it a popular choice for corporate branding. Additionally, blue is known to have a calming effect on the mind and body, which is why it is often used in spaces designed for relaxation and contemplation.

In nature, blue is a rare color, which can make it feel special and unique. The sky and ocean, both blue, are vast and seemingly infinite, which can inspire feelings of freedom and exploration. This connection to nature can enhance the positive feelings associated with the color blue.

Blue is a versatile color that can evoke a range of emotions and associations. It is often seen as a color of peace and tranquility, making it a popular choice for spaces designed for relaxation. Additionally, blue is frequently used in corporate branding to convey trust and reliability.
